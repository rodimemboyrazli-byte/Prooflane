# Prooflane logo sting

The frame is a quiet Proof-Paper field with Industry Ink typography and a rare
Signal-Teal motion accent. It must read as a precise, industrial evidence
system rather than a generic technology animation. Hairline guides and small
registration marks can support the proof-trace idea, but never compete with the
logo.

The three mark bars rise from a shared baseline in increasing height. Their
motion has controlled energy: brisk upward travel, a tiny overshoot, then a
firm settle. The complete wordmark is the final focal point. No gradients,
neon, glass, stock imagery, floating cards, or decorative claims.
